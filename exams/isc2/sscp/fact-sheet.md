# ISC2 SSCP (Systems Security Certified Practitioner) Fact Sheet

## Exam Overview

**Exam Code:** SSCP
**Exam Name:** SSCP - Systems Security Certified Practitioner
**Level:** Associate (practitioner)
**Duration:** 2 hours
**Format:** Computerized adaptive testing (CAT) for all exams; multiple choice and advanced item types
**Questions:** 100-125
**Passing Score:** 700 out of 1000
**Cost:** Not verified. The ISC2 SSCP page states no price (checked 2026-11-07); see the page for current bundles
**Valid For:** Not stated on the exam page or outline (checked 2026-11-07); maintenance (AMF and CPE) is set by ISC2
**Delivery:** Pearson testing center
**Languages:** English, Japanese, Spanish
**Prerequisites:** 1 year of required work experience (ISC2 page: "For professionals with 1+ year experience in security operations")

> **Verify before booking.** Everything above was read from the ISC2 pages on 2026-11-07. Price, maintenance fee and CPE totals were not on those pages, so they are left out rather than guessed.

**[📖 ISC2 SSCP certification page](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/sscp)** - name, required work experience, accreditation (ANAB, ISO/IEC 17024, DoDM 8140.03) and exam purchase options
**[📖 ISC2 SSCP exam outline](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/sscp/sscp-certification-exam-outline)** - the authoritative outline, effective October 1, 2025

## Who it is for

Hands-on practitioners: security administrators, systems and network administrators, SOC analysts and anyone who operates security controls day to day. SSCP is the practitioner rung of the ISC2 ladder, between [CC](../cc/) and the senior [CISSP](../cissp/) and [CCSP](../ccsp/).

## Exam Domains

Seven domains, effective **October 1, 2025**. AI security topics are embedded across the domains rather than listed as their own domain.

### Domain 1: Security Concepts and Practices (16%)

1.1 codes of ethics; 1.2 security concepts; 1.3 technical, physical and administrative controls and audits; 1.4 controls by type; 1.5 asset life cycle; 1.6 change and configuration management; 1.7 awareness and training; 1.8 collaboration with physical security.

### Domain 2: Access Controls (15%)

2.1 authentication methods; 2.2 internetwork trust architectures; 2.3 identity management life cycle; 2.4 access control models.

### Domain 3: Risk Identification, Monitoring and Analysis (15%)

3.1 risk management; 3.2 legal and regulatory concerns; 3.3 security assessments and vulnerability management; 3.4 operating and monitoring security platforms; 3.5 analyzing monitoring results.

### Domain 4: Incident Response and Recovery (14%)

4.1 incident response life cycle; 4.2 forensic investigations; 4.3 business continuity and disaster recovery planning.

### Domain 5: Cryptography (9%)

5.1 reasons and requirements for cryptography; 5.2 cryptography concepts; 5.3 secure protocols; 5.4 public key infrastructure.

### Domain 6: Network and Communications Security (16%)

6.1 networking fundamentals; 6.2 network attacks and countermeasures; 6.3 network access controls; 6.4 network security management; 6.5 security appliances and services; 6.6 wireless; 6.7 IoT.

### Domain 7: Systems and Application Security (15%)

7.1 malicious code and activity; 7.2 endpoint security; 7.3 mobile devices; 7.4 cloud security; 7.5 secure virtual environments.
