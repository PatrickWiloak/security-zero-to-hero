# IAPP AIGP Exam Strategy

- **Know the format.** 100 multiple-choice questions in 2.75 hours (IAPP FAQ, checked 2026-11-07), about 99 seconds each. Passing is 300 on a 100-500 scale with no section minimums, so a weak domain can be offset.
- **Read the verb.** The blueprint says the opening verb of each skill statement signals question difficulty. "Identify" and "define" are recall; "evaluate" and "implement" are judgment.
- **Find the role first.** Provider, deployer, importer or distributor? The obligation follows the role.
- **Find the stage next.** Is the scenario in design, training, release, deployment or monitoring? The right control differs at each stage.
- **Prefer documented and proportionate.** The best answer assigns an owner, records the decision and fits the risk. Reject answers that are extreme (ban everything) or informal (trust the vendor).
- **Law versus guidance.** A question that says "required" points to a law. "Voluntary" or "framework" points to NIST or OECD.
- **Use the ranges.** Domains III and IV are about half the exam together (21-25 questions each).
- **No new facts.** Answer from the stated scenario, not from what you think might also be true.
