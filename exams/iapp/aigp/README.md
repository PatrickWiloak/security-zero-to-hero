# IAPP AIGP - Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional

The credential for people who decide how an organization may use AI: which uses are allowed, who is accountable, what must be documented and when a system is switched off. It tests judgment about law, risk and process, not model building. For the engineering and safety side of the same topic see [AI safety at ai.noblerworks.com](https://ai.noblerworks.com/safety/).

## Exam Details

- **Exam Code:** AIGP
- **Duration:** 2.75 hours
- **Questions:** 100 multiple-choice, some scenario-based
- **Passing Score:** 300 on a 100-500 scale

## How the exam thinks

The blueprint says IAPP questions mostly sit at the remember/understand and apply/analyze levels, and that the opening verb of each skill statement (identify, evaluate, implement, define) signals how hard the question is. In practice you are asked what a governance professional should do given a situation, and the best answer is usually the one that is documented, proportionate, assigns an owner and involves the right cross-functional group. Role matters: whether your organization is the provider or the deployer changes the obligation.

## Study sequence

1. **Foundations** (Domain I) - vocabulary, risks, principles, roles. Short, and everything else leans on it.
2. **Laws, standards and frameworks** (Domain II) - the densest content; start it early and revisit.
3. **Governing development** (Domain III) - the life cycle from use case to release.
4. **Governing deployment and use** (Domain IV) - buying, configuring, monitoring and switching off.
