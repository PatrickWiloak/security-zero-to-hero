# IAPP AIGP Study Plan

Four weeks at 6-8 hours a week. IAPP recommends at least 30 hours of study in total. Study against the [v2.1 body of knowledge](https://iapp.org/certify/body-of-knowledge-aigp); the question ranges per domain set how much time each gets.

## Week 1: Foundations (Domain I, 16-20 questions)

- [ ] Read `notes/01-foundations-of-ai-governance.md`
- [ ] Read the EU AI Act definitions in [Article 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) and the [OECD principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles)
- [ ] Read NIST AI RMF Section 3 on trustworthy characteristics

## Week 2: Laws, standards and frameworks (Domain II, 19-23 questions)

- [ ] Read `notes/02-laws-standards-and-frameworks.md`
- [ ] Read Articles 5, 6, 9-15, 26, 50 and 99 of the AI Act and Annex III
- [ ] Read the GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE sections of the AI RMF

## Week 3: Development and deployment (Domains III and IV, 21-25 each)

- [ ] Read notes 03 and 04
- [ ] Read the [AI safety pillar](https://ai.noblerworks.com/safety/) for the security view of the same life cycle

## Week 4: Practice and review

- [ ] Work through the [practice questions](../../../resources/practice-questions/iapp-aigp.md); read every explanation
- [ ] Re-read the notes for any domain below 70%
- [ ] Work through the [scenarios](./scenarios.md)

## Readiness check

You are ready when you can explain, without notes, the difference between: provider and deployer, prohibited and high-risk, a framework and a law, GOVERN and MANAGE, model drift and data drift, and a policy and a control.
