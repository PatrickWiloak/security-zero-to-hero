# IAPP Certifications

## About IAPP

The International Association of Privacy Professionals (IAPP) runs the best-known credentials in privacy and, since the AIGP, in AI governance. Its exams are written by exam development boards and each one publishes a body of knowledge with an exam blueprint. Governance and privacy work overlap heavily, so people often hold the AIGP next to a privacy credential.
