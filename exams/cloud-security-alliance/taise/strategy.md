# CSA TAISE Exam Strategy

- **Know the format.** 60 multiple-choice questions, 120 minutes, online, open-book, unproctored (CSA, checked 2026-11-07). That is two minutes per question.
- **Pass mark.** 80% means 48 of 60. You can miss 12.
- **Equal modules.** Six questions per module. Do not neglect a module you find dull.
- **Open-book is not lookup-everything.** Keep a one-page index of your notes by module so you can find a term quickly.
- **Read the stem fully.** Many wrong answers are true statements that do not answer the question.
- **Prefer layered, risk-based answers.** Security and governance answers combine controls and tie to risk.
- **Two attempts are included** in the bundle; do not burn the first one as a trial.
