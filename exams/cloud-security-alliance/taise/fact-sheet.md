# CSA TAISE (Trusted AI Safety Expert Certificate) Fact Sheet

## Exam Overview

**Exam Code:** TAISE
**Exam Name:** Trusted AI Safety Expert (TAISE) Certificate
**Level:** Associate (knowledge certificate; basic familiarity with AI, cloud and security concepts recommended)
**Duration:** 120 minutes
**Format:** Online, open-book and unproctored (CSA launch article)
**Questions:** 60 multiple-choice, 6 per module across 10 modules
**Passing Score:** 80% (CSA education page)
**Cost:** US$795 for the TAISE Training + Exam Bundle, including two exam attempts (CSA education page, checked 2026-11-07). CSA sells it only as a bundle, with no exam-only option
**Valid For:** The two included exam attempts are valid for 2 years. The pages I read do not state how long the certificate itself is valid or any renewal requirement
**Delivery:** Online through CSA's training site; created by CSA with Northeastern University
**Languages:** Not stated on the pages read
**Prerequisites:** None required

> **Verify before booking.** Everything above was read from CSA's pages on 2026-11-07. The certificate launched in October 2025, so details may change; the prep kit's Knowledge Guide and FAQ (a download) hold the full exam format and were not read here.

## Who it is for

Security, risk, compliance and engineering staff who need a recognized grounding in AI safety and security across the AI life cycle. It is a knowledge-based certificate rather than an experience-based credential.

## Exam Domains

CSA organizes the curriculum into ten self-paced modules, and the exam has six questions per module, so the modules are weighted equally (10% each). The order below is CSA's.

| Module | Weight | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Introduction to AI | 10% | [01](./notes/01-introduction-and-generative-ai-architecture.md) |
| 2. Generative AI Architecture and Design | 10% | [01](./notes/01-introduction-and-generative-ai-architecture.md) |
| 3. Generative AI Use Cases and Applications | 10% | [02](./notes/02-use-cases-and-ethics.md) |
| 4. Ethics, Transparency, and Explainability in AI | 10% | [02](./notes/02-use-cases-and-ethics.md) |
| 5. AI Model Lifecycle and Threat Taxonomy | 10% | [03](./notes/03-lifecycle-threats-and-governance.md) |
| 6. Model Governance, Risk Management, and Compliance | 10% | [03](./notes/03-lifecycle-threats-and-governance.md) |
| 7. Introduction to AI Security and Safety | 10% | [04](./notes/04-ai-security-and-cloud.md) |
| 8. Cloud and AI Security | 10% | [04](./notes/04-ai-security-and-cloud.md) |
| 9. Data Security and Privacy AI Systems | 10% | [05](./notes/05-data-privacy-and-continuous-learning.md) |
| 10. Continuous Learning and Adaption | 10% | [05](./notes/05-data-privacy-and-continuous-learning.md) |

CSA's names are shown as published (including "Adaption"). CSA's launch article describes the topic areas slightly differently (for example "cloud security and incident response in AI environments"); the education page's module list is used here.
