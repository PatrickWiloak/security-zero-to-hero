# ISACA AAISM Study Plan

Four weeks at 6-8 hours a week for someone who already holds CISM or CISSP. Study against the [current outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaism/aaism-exam-content-outline).

## Week 1: Governance and program management (Domain 1, 31%)

- [ ] Read `notes/01-ai-governance-and-program-management.md`
- [ ] Skim the NIST AI RMF GOVERN function and the AI Act's Article 5 and Annex III headings
- [ ] Draft a one-page AI inventory template for your own organization

## Week 2: Risk and opportunity management (Domain 2, 31%)

- [ ] Read `notes/02-ai-risk-and-opportunity-management.md`
- [ ] Read the NIST AI 100-2 attack-goal summary and the OWASP LLM Top 10 list
- [ ] List the questions you would put in an AI vendor assessment

## Week 3: Technologies and controls (Domain 3, 38%)

- [ ] Read `notes/03-ai-technologies-and-controls.md`
- [ ] Read NIST AI 600-1 Sections 2.4, 2.9 and 2.12
- [ ] Sketch an AI security architecture with model, data, retrieval and tool layers

## Week 4: Practice and review

- [ ] Work through the [practice questions](../../../resources/practice-questions/isaca-aaism.md); read every explanation
- [ ] Re-read the notes for any domain below 70%
- [ ] Work through the [scenarios](./scenarios.md)

## Readiness check

You are ready when you can explain, without notes, the difference between: evasion and poisoning, prompt injection and jailbreaking, a model card and a data lineage record, governance and management, and risk appetite and risk threshold.
