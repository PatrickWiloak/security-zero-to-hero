# ISACA AAISM (Advanced in AI Security Management) Fact Sheet

## Exam Overview

**Exam Code:** AAISM
**Exam Name:** ISACA Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM)
**Level:** Advanced (add-on to CISM or CISSP)
**Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
**Questions:** 90 multiple-choice
**Passing Score:** 450 on a scaled 200-800 score (scored and pretest items; no penalty for wrong answers)
**Cost:** US$459 ISACA member / US$599 nonmember exam registration, plus a one-time US$50 application processing fee (ISACA candidate guide and certification page, checked 2026-11-07)
**Valid For:** Maintained annually: 10 CPE hours a year (30 over a 3-year period), annual maintenance fee US$20 member / US$35 nonmember (ISACA maintenance page, checked 2026-11-07; whether proof of an active CISM or CISSP is also checked at maintenance was not stated there, not verified)
**Delivery:** Computer-based at PSI testing centers or remotely proctored
**Languages:** English, Spanish and Japanese
**Prerequisites:** An active CISM or CISSP (ISC2) credential to be certified. No separate work-experience requirement is listed. The exam can be taken within a six-month eligibility window from registration, and candidates have five years from passing to apply for certification
**Content outline:** AAISM Examination Content Outline (the "June 2025" date used earlier is not stated on the outline page or candidate guide read 2026-11-07; the candidate guide is (c) 2025 ISACA and the page (c) 2026)

> **Verify before booking.** Everything above was read from ISACA's pages and exam candidate guide on 2026-11-07. Prices change; confirm on the registration page.

Retake policy (candidate guide): four attempts within a rolling 12 months; wait 30 days after the first attempt, then 90 days between later attempts; the full fee is paid for each attempt.

## Who it is for

Security professionals who already hold CISM or CISSP and now own the security of AI systems: securing enterprise AI solutions and using AI to improve security operations (ISACA's description). Pairs with [CISM](../cism/) and [ISC2 CISSP](../../isc2/cissp/).

## Exam Domains

| Domain | Weight | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. AI Governance and Program Management | 31% | [01](./notes/01-ai-governance-and-program-management.md) |
| 2. AI Risk and Opportunity Management | 31% | [02](./notes/02-ai-risk-and-opportunity-management.md) |
| 3. AI Technologies and Controls | 38% | [03](./notes/03-ai-technologies-and-controls.md) |

ISACA's candidate guide titles Domain 2 "AI Risk and Opportunity Management"; the outline web page shortens it to "AI Risk Management". The weights are the same.

### Domain 1 (31%)

1A stakeholder considerations, industry frameworks and regulatory requirements; 1B AI-related strategies, policies and procedures; 1C AI asset and data life cycle management; 1D AI security program development and management; 1E business continuity and incident response.

### Domain 2 (31%)

2A AI risk assessment, thresholds and treatment; 2B AI threat and vulnerability management; 2C AI vendor and supply chain management.

### Domain 3 (38%)

3A AI security architecture and design; 3B AI life cycle (for example model selection, training and validation); 3C data management controls; 3D privacy, ethical, trust and safety controls; 3E security controls and monitoring.
