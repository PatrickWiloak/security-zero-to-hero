# ISACA AAIA Study Plan

Four weeks at 6-8 hours a week for someone who already works in IT audit. Study against the [June 2025 outline](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/aaia/aaia-exam-content-outline). Domain 2 is 46%, so it gets the most time.

## Week 1: Governance and risk (Domain 1, 33%)

- [ ] Read `notes/01-ai-governance-and-risk.md`
- [ ] Skim the NIST AI RMF GOVERN and MAP functions and the EU AI Act risk tiers
- [ ] List what an auditor would ask for to confirm AI ownership and accountability

## Week 2: AI operations part 1 (Domain 2, 46%)

- [ ] Read the first half of `notes/02-ai-operations.md` (2A to 2D)
- [ ] Draw the AI solution life cycle and mark where a control and an audit test belong

## Week 3: AI operations part 2 and audit techniques

- [ ] Read the second half of note 02 (2E to 2G) and `notes/03-ai-auditing-tools-and-techniques.md`
- [ ] Write a short audit test plan for one control, with sample size reasoning

## Week 4: Practice and review

- [ ] Work through the [practice questions](../../../resources/practice-questions/isaca-aaia.md); read every explanation
- [ ] Re-read the notes for any domain below 70%
- [ ] Work through the [scenarios](./scenarios.md)

## Readiness check

You are ready when you can explain, without notes, the difference between: design and operating effectiveness, a control and a control objective, statistical and judgmental sampling, validation and verification, and model drift and data drift.
