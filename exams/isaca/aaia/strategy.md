# ISACA AAIA Exam Strategy

- **Know the format.** 90 multiple-choice questions in 150 minutes, about 100 seconds each. Passing is 450 on a scaled 200-800 score; ISACA states there is a mix of scored and pretest items and that domain results are informational only.
- **Weight your time.** Domain 2 is 46%, Domain 1 is 33%, Domain 3 is 21%.
- **Be the auditor.** The best answer evaluates, requests evidence, or reports. It rarely has the auditor building or operating the control.
- **Risk first.** Audit scope and depth follow risk. Pick the answer that targets the highest-risk area first.
- **Evidence quality.** Prefer evidence that is independent, reliable and obtained directly over assertions.
- **Independence.** Be wary of answers that compromise auditor independence.
- **Design then operation.** First check a control is designed to meet the objective, then test that it operated.
- **Answer from the stem.** Use the facts given.
