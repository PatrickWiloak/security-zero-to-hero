# ISACA AAIA (Advanced in AI Audit) Fact Sheet

## Exam Overview

**Exam Code:** AAIA
**Exam Name:** ISACA Advanced in AI Audit (AAIA)
**Level:** Advanced (add-on to CISA or an equivalent audit credential)
**Duration:** 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
**Questions:** 90 multiple-choice
**Passing Score:** 450 on a scaled 200-800 score (scored and pretest items)
**Cost:** US$459 ISACA member / US$599 nonmember exam registration, plus a one-time US$50 application processing fee (ISACA certification page and candidate guide, checked 2026-11-07)
**Valid For:** Maintained annually: 10 CPE hours a year in AI (30 over 3 years), annual maintenance fee US$20 member / US$35 nonmember, and an active prerequisite credential (ISACA maintenance page, checked 2026-11-07)
**Delivery:** Computer-based at PSI testing centers or remotely proctored (remote proctoring is not available in India, Mainland China and Hong Kong)
**Languages:** English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Japanese
**Prerequisites:** An active CISA to be certified, or one of the qualifying credentials the candidate guide lists (for example CIA, US CPA, ACCA, ICAEW) together with an IT audit or IT advisory role. No separate work-experience requirement is listed. Six-month exam eligibility window from registration; five years from passing to apply for certification
**Content outline:** AAIA Examination Content Outline, June 2025

> **Verify before booking.** Everything above was read from ISACA's pages and candidate guide on 2026-11-07. Confirm prices and the qualifying credential list before booking.

Retake policy (candidate guide): four attempts within a rolling 12 months; wait 30 days after the first attempt and 90 days between later attempts.

## Who it is for

IT audit professionals who need to provide assurance over AI-driven processes (ISACA's description) and to use AI inside the audit process. Pairs with [CISA](../cisa/) and complements [AAISM](../aaism/) on the security-management side.

## Exam Domains

| Domain | Weight | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. AI Governance and Risk | 33% | [01](./notes/01-ai-governance-and-risk.md) |
| 2. AI Operations | 46% | [02](./notes/02-ai-operations.md) |
| 3. AI Auditing Tools and Techniques | 21% | [03](./notes/03-ai-auditing-tools-and-techniques.md) |

### Domain 1 (33%)

1A AI models, considerations and requirements; 1B AI governance and program management; 1C AI risk management; 1D privacy and data governance programs; 1E leading practices, ethics, regulations and standards for AI.

### Domain 2 (46%)

2A data management specific to AI; 2B AI solution development methodologies and life cycle; 2C change management specific to AI; 2D supervision of AI solutions (outputs, impacts and decisions); 2E testing techniques for AI solutions; 2F threats and vulnerabilities specific to AI; 2G incident response management specific to AI.

### Domain 3 (21%)

3A audit planning and design; 3B audit testing and sampling methodologies; 3C audit evidence collection techniques; 3D audit data quality and data analytics; 3E AI audit outputs and reports.
